# Blackjack Navigator

A blackjack trainer: play real hands against a correct engine, with a coach that grades every decision against basic strategy for the table you are sitting at and explains why.

**Play:** https://akuforge.com/blackjack/

On an iPhone, open the link in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. It runs full-screen and works offline after the first visit. Your chips, stats and history stay on your own device. Play money only.
